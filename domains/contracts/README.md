# WEALTH — Domain: Contracts Law

> **Subdomain of `domains/law/`. Canonical ontology: `domains/law/ONTOLOGY.yaml`.**
> **Canonical canon: `canon/002_HUMAN_LAW.md` + `canon/002a_LAW_SHADOW.md`.**

This subdomain covers enforceable promises as capital geometry: formation, performance, breach, remedies, and limitation.

## Primary canonical sources

| Act | Jurisdiction | Priority | Notes |
|-----|--------------|----------|-------|
| Contracts Act 1950 | MY-FED | P0 | Formation, consideration, capacity, consent |
| Specific Relief Act 1950 | MY-FED | P1 | Specific performance, injunctions |
| Limitation Act 1953 | MY-FED | P1 | Time-decay on remedies |

## Key institutions

- **Mahkamah Tinggi (Sivil)** — contract disputes, specific performance.
- **Mahkamah Rayuan / Mahkamah Persekutuan** — appeal paths.

## Shadow notes

- "Legally binding" and "commercially wise" are different geometries.
- Oral agreements may be enforceable but harder to prove.
- Remedies are optionality, not certainty.

## Anti-patterns

- Telling a user "this contract is valid" — verdict language.
- Ignoring capacity / undue influence / public policy flags.
