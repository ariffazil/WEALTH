# WEALTH — Domain: Land Law

> **Subdomain of `domains/law/`. Canonical ontology: `domains/law/ONTOLOGY.yaml`.**
> **Canonical canon: `canon/002_HUMAN_LAW.md` + `canon/002a_LAW_SHADOW.md`.**

This subdomain covers land as capital geometry in Malaysia — title, transfer, lease, charge, easement, Malay reservation, and state alienation.

## Primary canonical sources

| Act | Jurisdiction | Priority | Notes |
|-----|--------------|----------|-------|
| Kanun Tanah Negara 1965 | MY-FED / MY-STATE | P0 | Core land code for Peninsular Malaysia |
| State Land Rules (Penang) | MY-PG | P0 | Penang-specific alienation and title |
| Federal Constitution — Articles 13, 89, 153 | MY-FED | P0 | Property rights, Malay reservation, special position |

## Key institutions

- **PTG Pulau Pinang** — land registration, transfer approval (Borang 14A).
- **Mahkamah Tinggi (Sivil)** — probate / administration affecting land.
- **Mahkamah Syariah** — Muslim estate matters touching land.

## Shadow notes

- PTG counters have real-world delay and backlog.
- Malay reservation land carries constitutional weight (Article 89).
- Adat may restrict family disposition even where KTN permits transfer.

## Anti-patterns

- "This land is definitely transferable" — verdict language. Use advisory + flags.
- Silent jurisdiction (Federal vs State vs Syariah).
- Ignoring dignity risk when family pusaka is involved.
