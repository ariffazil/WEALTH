# WEALTH — Domain: Regulatory Law

> **Subdomain of `domains/law/`. Canonical ontology: `domains/law/ONTOLOGY.yaml`.**
> **Canonical canon: `canon/002_HUMAN_LAW.md` + `canon/002a_LAW_SHADOW.md`.**

This subdomain covers regulatory constraints on capital action: personal data, environment, safety, licensing, and sector-specific rules.

## Primary canonical sources

| Act | Jurisdiction | Priority | Notes |
|-----|--------------|----------|-------|
| Personal Data Protection Act 2010 | MY-FED | P0 | Cross-cutting — every record involving a natural person |
| Environmental Quality Act 1974 | MY-FED | P1 | Land / project impact |
| Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 | MY-FED | P1 | Workplace / project safety |

## Key institutions

- **Personal Data Protection Commissioner** — PDPA enforcement.
- **Department of Environment (DOE)** — EIA / environmental approvals.
- **Department of Occupational Safety and Health (DOSH)** — safety compliance.

## Shadow notes

- PDPA consent cannot be assumed; explicit human acknowledgement required.
- Regulatory breaches can block land deals, corporate actions, and data processing.

## Anti-patterns

- Storing heir personal data without `consent.obtained`.
- Treating regulatory check as a one-time event rather than ongoing constraint.
