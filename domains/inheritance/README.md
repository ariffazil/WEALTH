# WEALTH — Domain: Inheritance / Pusaka

> **Subdomain of `domains/law/`. Canonical ontology: `domains/law/ONTOLOGY.yaml`.**
> **Canonical canon: `canon/002_HUMAN_LAW.md` + `canon/002a_LAW_SHADOW.md`.**

This subdomain covers inheritance as capital re-allocation under rule: Muslim estates (faraid), non-Muslim intestate estates, probate, small estates, and the living-human gaps around them.

## Primary canonical sources

| Act | Jurisdiction | Priority | Notes |
|-----|--------------|----------|-------|
| Small Estates (Distribution) Act 1955 | MY-FED | P0 | Non-Muslim, estate ≤ RM 2M, simplified route |
| Probate and Administration Act 1959 | MY-FED | P0 | Non-Muslim, estate > RM 2M or with will |
| Distribution Act 1958 | MY-FED | P0 | Non-Muslim intestate shares |
| Faraid (Syariah) | MY-SYR | P0 | Muslim fixed shares — Quran, Sunnah, Ijmak, Qiyas |
| Kanun Tanah Negara 1965 s.214 | MY-STATE | P0 | Transfer by personal representative |

## Key institutions

- **Mahkamah Tinggi (Sivil)** — probate / SKM for non-Muslims.
- **Mahkamah Syariah** — Sijil Faraid for Muslims.
- **JKPTG / PTG** — small estates distribution and land transfer.
- **Amanah Raya Berhad** — executor appointment / asset management.

## Shadow notes

- Most Malay land disputes are inheritance-related.
- The RM 2M boundary between small estates and probate is a shadow threshold.
- Family adat may conflict with statutory distribution.

## Anthropology / dignity signals

- Widow who does not know Small Estates route exists.
- Minor heir present → 888_HOLD mandatory.
- Disputed will → escalate to peguam.
- Faraid result → counsel review mandatory.

## Anti-patterns

- Computing shares without declaring religion boundary.
- Issuing distribution as "verdict" rather than advisory.
- Ignoring adat / family agreement tension.
