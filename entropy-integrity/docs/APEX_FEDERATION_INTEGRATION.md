### 3. Architecture Hardening: The Ten Axioms in Action

1.  **State-vs-Trait Enforcement:** The WELL and KERNEL components strip diagnostic/trait classifications (`narcissist`, `evil`) from all input streams, routing text through a non-punitive mirror.
2.  **Telemetry over Metrics:** The trajectory detector (`trajectory.py`) tracks the rate of change in correctability rather than simple static scores, preventing Goodhart's Law from corrupting the indicators.
3.  **Governance Separation:** All A2A tasks must write signed artifacts before execution, decoupling evidence generation from final adjudication.
