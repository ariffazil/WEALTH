## 3. Board Appointment Patterns

### IDEAS Pantau Kuasa Data (Political Appointments)

| Administration | Political Appointments Detected | FSB Chairpersons Politically Appointed |
|---------------|-------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| Najib Razak (PM-6) | 301 | Highest rate |
| Ismail Sabri (PM-9) | 273 (in 15 months) | High |
| Muhyiddin Yassin (PM-8) | 186 (in 17 months) | High |
| Anwar Ibrahim (PM-10) | 95 (as of Oct 2024) | 78% of FSB chairpersons |
| Mahathir Mohamad (PM-7) | Lowest GLC appointments (13) | Lower |

**Key Findings:**
- IDEAS tracked 135 FSBs and 283 GLCs across 5 administrations
- Audit (Accounts of Companies) Order 2017 lists up to 1,858 GLCs total
- Under Anwar: 24 politicians hold 26 positions in 21 GLCs/GLICs under MoF Inc (~6% of key roles)
- UMNO (22 appointments) has nearly as many as PKR + Amanah combined (24)
- Key multiple appointees: PM Anwar Ibrahim, Minister Rafizi Ramli, DPM Ahmad Zahid Hamidi
- Malaysian Code on Corporate Governance (MCCG) Guidance 5.8 discourages active politicians on boards
- PM Anwar's position: "All appointments must be professional, but political party position should not be an issue"

**Board Composition Patterns (General):**
- Chairpersons: Predominantly politically connected or retired civil servants
- Independent directors: Typically 2-3 per board (minimum required by Bursa Listing Requirements)
- CEO/executive directors: Mix of professional bankers/corporate executives and politically-connected appointees
- Bumiputera representation targets maintained per NEP/NDP legacy
- Financial institutions (Maybank, CIMB, etc.) tend to have more professional/less overtly political boards

## 4. Government Procurement (ePerolehan) Concentration

### ePerolehan Platform Stats (2024)
- Total eP-registered suppliers: 118,298 (MOF-certified) + 5,566 (basic accounts) + 155 (G2G)
- Procurement value through ePerolehan in 2024: ~RM31.4 billion
- This is a subset of total government spending (~RM407 billion annually); much procurement happens outside ePerolehan

### Procurement by Ministry (2024, top spenders on ePerolehan)

| Ministry | Procurement Value (RM) | Share |
|----------|----------------------|-------|
| Ministry of Health (Kesihatan) | RM8.64 billion | 27.5% |
| Ministry of Defence (Pertahanan) | RM7.02 billion | 22.4% |
| Ministry of Education (Pendidikan) | RM4.68 billion | 14.9% |
| Ministry of Internal Security (Dalam Negeri) | RM3.84 billion | 12.2% |
| Prime Minister's Department | RM1.11 billion | 3.5% |
| All other ministries | RM6.15 billion | 19.6% |
| **Total** | **RM31.37 billion** | **100%** |

### Procurement Structure

| Method | Threshold | Description |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| Direct Purchase | Up to RM20,000 | Small everyday purchases |
| Sebut Harga (Quotation) | RM20,000 - RM500,000 | Invited quotes from registered suppliers |
| Open Tender | Above RM500,000 | Publicly advertised; price + capability evaluated |
| Direct Negotiation | Special approval only | Single-party award (proprietary/urgent) |

### GLC Procurement Concentration
- "G-15" GLCs estimated procurement spend: ~RM55 billion per annum
- TNB alone spent RM16.9 billion (1995-2019) to 822 companies (32 suppliers, 790 contractors)
- Major construction GLCs (Gamuda, IJM, UEM, Sunway Construction, WCT) dominate large infrastructure tenders
- Bumiputera preference: Higher-value tenders give preference to Bumiputera-registered firms
- Direct negotiation awards published for transparency but still represent concentrated allocation

## 5. Key Structural Observations

1. **Capital Concentration:** 6 GLICs managing RM2.2 trillion effectively control ~27% of Bursa market cap
2. **GEAR-uP Reform:** First systematic attempt to mandate 7.5% TSR across 37 GLCs with non-financial KPIs (living wages, talent development)
3. **Political Appointments Declining But Persistent:** 95 under Anwar vs 301 under Najib, but 78% of FSB chairpersons still politically connected (IDEAS)
4. **Procurement Fragmentation:** ePerolehan captures only ~RM31B of ~RM407B annual govt spending; massive procurement happens through GLC channels outside ePerolehan
5. **DDI Acceleration:** RM20.3B domestic deployment in 2025 (3x vs 2024's RM6.6B), with Khazanah leading at RM7.9B
6. **Market Cap Growth:** RM538B combined GLIC market cap at end-2024, up RM111B (25.8%) from 2023
