## What it does

WEALTH provides **capital intelligence** — resource allocation reasoning under uncertainty. It asks not just "is it technically correct?" but "is it worth doing?"

### Capital Primitives (Deductive Math)

- **NPV** — Net present value of cash flows
- **IRR** — Internal rate of return
- **EMV** — Expected monetary value under uncertainty
- **EVOI** — Expected value of information
- **Monte Carlo** — Stochastic simulation with 1000+ realizations
- **Kelly** — Optimal bet sizing under known odds
- **Markowitz** — Portfolio optimization with risk-return tradeoff
