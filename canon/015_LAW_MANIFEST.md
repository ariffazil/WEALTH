## Law Invariants (F2 TRUTH)

1. **Law has authority, not truth.** A statute can be unjust and still binding.
   WEALTH computes the geometry; arifOS judges whether to obey or escalate.
2. **Jurisdictions don't overlap silently.** Every action sits inside one
   jurisdiction (or a clearly-marked conflict). WEALTH must declare which.
3. **Immutability via amendment.** No silent rewrites. When a section is
   superseded, `superseded_by` is the arrow of time.
4. **Religious boundaries are constitutional.** Article 121(1A) —
   civil-Syariah separation. WEALTH must not suggest mixing.
5. **Advisory ≠ verdict.** Every WEALTH law output carries:
   `verdict_class ∈ {OBSERVE, ANALYZE, DRAFT}`, never `SEAL`.
6. **Maruah floor binds.** Any family matter, minor's share, religious
   boundary, or contested case → 888_HOLD mandatory.
7. **No case law in v0.** LLM cannot interpret precedents reliably.
   Provisions only — counsel interprets.
