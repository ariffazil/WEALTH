## 1. The Law

> **Law is capital geometry. No value without jurisdiction.**

Every unit of capital — money, land, title, right, claim, duty, inheritance, share, licence — exists **only** within a jurisdiction. The jurisdiction is the coordinate system. The law is the metric. Move the coordinates wrong, the geometry distorts, the capital vanishes.

WEALTH does not treat law as "compliance overhead". WEALTH treats law as the **imaginary layer** that makes the real layer (capital) measurable. Without law, money has no title, land has no owner, inheritance has no heir, and risk has no remedy.

This is the void the canon fills.

## 4. The Two Iron Rules

### Rule 1 — WEALTH never issues binding legal verdicts.

Every legal tool emits **ADVISORY + FLAGS**. The advisory tells the human what the law appears to say, the jurisdiction it lives in, and the risk class. The flag tells arifOS/AAA which floor must clear before action.

A verdict ("this is illegal", "you must do X") is **HARAM** at WEALTH. Verdicts belong to:
- The human (888) for personal matters.
- The court for binding orders.
- arifOS for constitutional gating.

WEALTH is the geometry. It does not judge the geometry.

### Rule 2 — Every legal tool is receipted.

Every call:
- Emits a receipt (tier T1 minimum) to VAULT999.
- Tags jurisdiction (`MY-FED`, `MY-PG`, `MY-SBH`, `MY-SWK`, `MY-SYR`, etc.).
- Declares risk class (`LOW` / `MEDIUM` / `HIGH` / `CRITICAL`).
- Names the human floor required (`888_REVIEW` for HIGH/CRITICAL).
- Cites the source (Act, section, gazette date, version).

Silent legal action is impossible. The receipt is the contract.

## 6. Risk Classes (Constitutional Binding)

Every legal tool call classifies its action into one of four classes:

| Class | Meaning | Required witness | Default floor |
|-------|---------|------------------|---------------|
| **LOW** | Lookup, classification, no decision | None | Auto |
| **MEDIUM** | Advisory with downstream impact | arifOS audit trail | 888 sign-off on action |
| **HIGH** | Affects rights, titles, inheritance, money | 888 explicit | 888_HOLD + human review |
| **CRITICAL** | Constitutional / cross-border / irreversible | 888 + AAA consensus | 888_HOLD + epoch seal |

A `LOW` lookup never needs 888. A `CRITICAL` advice (e.g. "this transfer may void MA63 protections") is escalated to 888 and AAA before any action.

## 8. Anti-Patterns (Forbidden)

These fail the canon on receipt:

1. **Verdict drift** — tool says "this is illegal". Fix: emit ADVISORY + flag, never verdict.
2. **Silent jurisdiction** — tool omits jurisdiction tag. Fix: every record carries `jurisdiction` field or fails.
3. **Unsourced claim** — tool cites an Act without section and gazette date. Fix: source schema is mandatory.
4. **Cross-jurisdiction leap** — tool mixes Federal + State + Syariah in one advisory without separation. Fix: emit one advisory per jurisdiction, then a synthesis.
5. **Self-judging** — tool outputs a binding course of action. Fix: hand off to 888 + arifOS.
6. **Hantu drift** — tool claims to "understand" the user's legal situation with empathy. Fix: advisory, not counsel.
7. **Live case contamination** — tool imports pending case text into advisory. Fix: forbid `outcomes.jsonl` mixing with law pack; separate ledger.
8. **Unwitnessed HIGH/CRITICAL** — tool returns HIGH without `888_HOLD` flag. Fix: tool returns HOLD, not answer.
